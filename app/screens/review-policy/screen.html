Review policy

Reviews on SolarSaral come only from customers who requested a survey through us, at real milestones.

When you can review

The survey review opens once the vendor marks the survey done and you confirm it. Installation reviews need evidence (an invoice number or photo). Long-term reviews need a generation reading.

DISCOM delays and subsidy-portal delays are shown on the review but excluded from the vendor's score, because they are outside the vendor's control.

Publishing

Every review — positive or negative — goes through the same 72-hour check and is then published. Negative reviews are never auto-hidden.

  • You can mark a review "Resolved by vendor" if they fixed the problem. The tag is shown publicly.

Sponsorship

Paying for the sponsored slot has no effect on reviews, ratings or accuracy.
